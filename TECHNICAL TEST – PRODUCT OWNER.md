# **TECHNICAL TEST – PRODUCT OWNER**

### **Case: E-Office**

Perusahaan berencana mengembangkan **E-Office**, yaitu sistem informasi untuk **kepegawaian, surat-menyurat elektronik, dan tata usaha terpusat** guna meningkatkan efisiensi staf.

Sebagai Product Owner, Anda diminta menyusun kebutuhan awal produk berdasarkan informasi tersebut.

## **Deliverables**

### **1\. Product Proposal**

Berisi:

* Problem Statement  
* Product Objective  
* Target User  
* Product Scope  
* Success Metrics

### **2\. BRD**

Berisi:

* Business Requirements  
* Stakeholder  
* Business Rules  
* Assumption  
* Constraint  
* Risk

### **3\. PRD**

Berisi:

* Product Overview  
* User Persona  
* Feature List  
* User Flow  
* MVP & Prioritization

### **4\. FSD / SRS**

Dokumentasikan minimal **5 fitur utama**, mencakup:

* Functional Requirement  
* Actor  
* Main Flow  
* Alternative/Exception Flow  
* Validation  
* Business Rules  
* Acceptance Criteria

### **5\. Product Backlog**

Minimal **15 User Stories**, mencakup:

* Epic  
* User Story  
* Priority  
* Story Point  
* Acceptance Criteria

### **6\. Supporting Documents**

Buat seperlunya:

* Role & Permission Matrix  
* ERD / Data Model  
* UAT Scenario  
* Risk Register  
* Product Roadmap

### **7\. Prototype**

Buat low-fidelity/high-fidelity prototype minimal:

* Login  
* Dashboard  
* Employee Management  
* Letter Management  
* Approval
